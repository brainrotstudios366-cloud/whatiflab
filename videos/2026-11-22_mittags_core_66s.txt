FACTS / SOURCES:
Core radius ~3,480 km (Mars 3,390 km); inner core ~5,200 °C; core ≈ 32% of Earth's mass (Britannica, National Geographic, Live Science)
Surface gravity scales with mass -> ~67.5% => 70 kg feels like ~47 kg, jumps ~1.5x higher
Geodynamo: magnetic field made by swirling liquid iron in the outer core
Speed of gravity = speed of light (GW170817, LIGO/Virgo 2017); Moon ≈ 1.28 light-seconds away
Earth "rings like a bell" for weeks after huge quakes (2004 Sumatra, NBC/Scientific American)
Free fall at core edge (g≈10.7 m/s²): ~130 m in 5 s; collapse energy is far more than enough to melt the planet (estimate)
